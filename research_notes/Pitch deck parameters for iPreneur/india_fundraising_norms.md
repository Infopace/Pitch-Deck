# Indian Fundraising Norms and Investor Deck Expectations (2025–2026), for iPreneur

Research date: 27 Oct 2026. All figures are dated in place. Most sources are news write-ups of reports (Bain-IVCA, Tracxn, EY-IVCA) rather than the full PDFs. Advisory and law-firm blogs are flagged where used.

---

## 1. Indian funding landscape 2025–2026: deal volumes, round sizes, valuations, active investor types

### Takeaway
After two years of growth, India VC reached about $16B in 2025 (Bain-IVCA, >1,300 deals, +18% deal count). Tracxn counts tech-startup funding more narrowly, at $10.5B (−17%), with seed down 30% and early stage the only stage that grew. H1 2026 PE/VC investment fell sharply. In Jan–Aug 2026, median rounds were roughly ₹8 Cr seed, ₹51 Cr Series A and ₹124 Cr Series B. Investors say the Series A bar "has gone up pretty significantly."

### Cited Findings
**Aggregate volumes**
- Bain & Company / IVCA *India Venture Capital Report 2026* (released ~4 Apr 2026): India VC/growth investment was about **$16B in 2025**, the second straight year of growth. Growth was "more balanced across deal volume and average deal size." — [The Tribune / ANI](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/fintech-saas-drive-indias-vc-investments-to-16-bn-in-2025-log-second-straight-year-of-growth-bain-report/)
- Same report: deal count rose **~18% YoY to >1,300 transactions**, driven broadly by deals **below $50M**. — [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/?p=95007); [Millennium Post](https://www.millenniumpost.in/business/defying-global-slowdown-india-vc-investments-jump-to-16-bn-in-2025-654324)
- Same report, sectors: **fintech** deal value more than doubled YoY. **Software/SaaS** funding rose ~1.5x. **AI/GenAI-native** funding rose 1.5x. — [The Tribune / ANI](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/fintech-saas-drive-indias-vc-investments-to-16-bn-in-2025-log-second-straight-year-of-growth-bain-report/)
- Same report, exits: ~**$2B in IPO exits** in 2025, mainly from eight VC-backed listings. The 2026 outlook is a "**monetization-led phase**" with priority on capital efficiency and clear exit paths. — [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/vcs-see-2b-from-ipo-exits-in-2025/story); [Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/business/indias-vc-landscape-to-shift-to-monetization-profitability-by-2026-articleshow-gqgt3n1)
- Tracxn full-year 2025, India **tech** startups: **$10.5B total (−17% YoY)**. **Investor types active**

- Peak XV Surge 12: **13 of 18** companies target **global** markets, not India. **50–60%** of a typical cohort comes from operating roles at established tech companies. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/peak-xv-goes-bigger-at-seed-with-new-surge-cohort-as-series-a-bar-rises/)

### Inferences
- Implied FX in FinVal's 2026 conversions is **~₹95–96/USD** (₹50.8 Cr ≈ $5.3M; ₹123.8 Cr ≈ $13.0M). iPreneur should store an explicit, dated INR/USD rate per deck rather than hard-code one.
- Suggested stage-default ranges for iPreneur, from the FinVal medians and IQR plus the Surge ceiling:
  - Pre-seed/angel: ~₹50 lakh–₹3 Cr
  - Seed: ₹2.4–19 Cr (median ~₹8 Cr)
  - Series A: ~₹50 Cr median
  - Series B: ~₹125 Cr median

  These are medians, not targets. Deep-tech and AI rounds skew higher.
- Because seed money contracted and the Series A bar rose, decks should front-load traction and capital-efficiency evidence, even at seed.
- The H1 2026 drop (EY-IVCA) and the "monetization-led" outlook (Bain) point the same way: decks in late 2026 face a tougher, profitability-minded audience.

### Gaps
- **India-specific valuation medians by stage (pre/post-money)** were not available in text. FinVal's chart is an image, and only 39% of deals disclose valuation. Dilution norms by stage in India: no reliable source found.
- Named tier-1 VC activity in 2025–26 (Accel, Elevation, Blume, Z47 (formerly Matrix India), Lightspeed India): I found no deal-count leaderboard in this session. The Bain full report's top-investor list was not in the news coverage.
- CVC activity and specific micro-VC fund sizes were not researched in depth.
- 100X.VC terms (iSAFE, cheque size) were not found. A targeted search returned nothing.
- Antler India's programme cheque size was not found.

## 2. What Indian VCs and angel networks explicitly ask for in decks (published templates/checklists)

### Takeaway
No major Indian investor (IAN, LetsVenture, Mumbai Angels, Blume, Peak XV, 100X.VC, Antler) turned up a public, official slide-by-slide template in this research. The only formal "pitch deck required" process found is the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) incubator application. Indian practitioner guidance converges on a 10–15 slide core deck, with an India-specific emphasis on verifiable financials: GST filings, MCA filings and bank statements, plus explicit unit economics.

### Cited Findings
- A YourStory playbook (Jan 2026) says most funded decks in India follow a **10–12 slide** structure. The core question is "is this business likely to work, and can this team execute it?" — [YourStory, Jan 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/01/pitch-deck-playbook-get-funded)
- The same playbook says projections must be "ambitious but believable" with stated assumptions: **CAC, pricing, conversion rates, churn, burn rate, runway, path to profitability**. It calls a vague ask like "We are raising Rs 2 to 3 crore" weak, so the ask should be precise. — [YourStory, Jan 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/01/pitch-deck-playbook-get-funded)
- Standard Indian angel deck must-haves: cover, problem, solution, **market sizing with sources**, business model, traction, go-to-market, competition, team. Angels weigh problem-market fit, team credibility, early traction, monetisation clarity and **exit potential**. — [TheCEO.in](https://www.theceo.in/blogs/fundraising-101-pitch-startup-angel-investors-india)
- Indian diligence practice: state whether revenue figures are **net of GST**. Revenue is cross-checked against **GST filings, MCA-filed financial statements and bank statements**. — [Legal Suvidha](https://legalsuvidha.com/blog/crafting-pitch-deck-for-startup) *(advisory blog)*
- An Indian VC checklist lists **positive contribution margin** and **CAC payback within 12–18 months** among the factors evaluated before a term sheet. — [Legal Suvidha](https://legalsuvidha.com/blog/15-things-vcs-look-for-in-your-company) *(practitioner blog, not data)*
- **SISFS application process:** the startup applies to up to **3 preferred incubators**. The incubator holds a virtual meeting with a **pitch deck presentation plus Q&A**. Documents commonly listed: DPIIT certificate, incorporation certificate, **business plan and pitch deck**, founder KYC (Aadhaar, PAN), revenue model, market research, financial projections, and sometimes a board resolution and a short product video. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1040488); [Startupflora](https://startupflora.com/blog/documents-required-for-sisfs); [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/startup-india-seed-fund-scheme) *(third-party guides; no official template found)*
- Typical Indian deck-service providers cite a "**10 to 15 slide**" deck. — [IncorpX](https://www.incorpx.io/investment-pitch-deck-in-uttar-pradesh) *(promotional, low weight)*
- Peak XV Surge founders are typically repeat entrepreneurs, experienced operators, or deeply technical founders. Team credentials carry heavy weight at seed. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/peak-xv-goes-bigger-at-seed-with-new-surge-cohort-as-series-a-bar-rises/)

### Inferences
- India-specific sections iPreneur should add beyond a generic US template:
  1. **Unit economics / contribution margin** slide (CM1/CM2/CM3 waterfall common in Indian consumer and D2C)
  2. **Path to profitability / burn and runway** slide
  3. **Compliance and structure** appendix slide: DPIIT, entity type, cap table, domicile
  4. **Use of funds in ₹** with runway months
  5. **Exit potential** slide: Indian IPO route now credible given 2025 IPO exits and reverse flips
- Project memory says iPreneur decks target **20–30 slides**, while Indian norms are 10–15 core slides. A **main deck plus an appendix** (financial detail, compliance, team bios, data room index) reconciles the two.
- Since revenue is reconciled to GST and MCA filings, iPreneur's financial inputs could include a "revenue basis" field (gross vs net of GST; FY basis) and a "verified against" flag.

### Gaps
- No official published deck checklist found for **IAN, LetsVenture, Mumbai Angels, Blume, Peak XV Surge, Accel, Elevation, Antler India, or 100X.VC**. Their websites were not fetched in this session. The next step would be fetching each application form directly (e.g. LetsVenture's founder application fields, 100X.VC's application).
- No official SISFS pitch deck template from startupindia.gov.in was located. Only third-party descriptions.

## 3. Metrics emphasis in India: profitability, unit economics, India-specific market sizing, SaaS-to-global

### Takeaway
Since 2022 the Indian investor lens has shifted from growth at all costs to **profitability, unit economics and cash-flow visibility**. Bain's 2026 outlook calls this a "monetization-led" phase. Market sizing must reckon with the fact that India's real consuming class is only ~140M people (~10% of the population, per Blume Indus Valley 2025), so "1.4 billion Indians" TAMs are a red flag. Digital public infrastructure (UPI at ~24.5B transactions/month by Aug 2026) is a credible distribution and market-sizing anchor.

### Cited Findings
- The Bain-IVCA report says investors have moved away from growth at all costs toward **profitability, unit economics and cash-flow visibility**, and growth-stage VCs are prioritising **capital efficiency and faster monetisation**. — [Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/business/indias-vc-market-hits-16b-focus-shifts-to-ai-and-profitability-articleshow-dg0nkvs); [Asianet Newsable (2026 outlook)](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/business/indias-vc-landscape-to-shift-to-monetization-profitability-by-2026-articleshow-gqgt3n1)
- At least **one-third of 2025's new unicorns** are profitable or near breakeven. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/explainers/mega-deals-new-unicorns-how-growth-stage-start-ups-are-fueling-indias-vc-comeback-in-2025)
- Practitioner thresholds: **positive contribution margin**, **CAC payback 12–18 months**. — [Legal Suvidha](https://legalsuvidha.com/blog/15-things-vcs-look-for-in-your-company) *(blog)*
- Blume Ventures analyst: "We like startups that become harder to replace the longer they exist." This points to retention and stickiness metrics. — [TheCEO.in](https://www.theceo.in/blogs/what-investors-really-want-insights-from-top-vc-firms-in-india)
- **Blume Indus Valley Annual Report 2025** — consuming class and India-1/2/3 segmentation:
  - India's consuming class is **~140M people (~10% of population), ~30M households**. — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1079648/scroll_in)
  - An "**aspirant**" class of ~300M sits below it. Around **1 billion** Indians lack income for discretionary spend. — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1079648/scroll_in)
  - Business Standard reports a much smaller "emerging/aspirant" figure (~30M), so the sources conflict on the aspirant segment. — [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/india-news/india-wealth-gap-consumption-study-2025-blume-125022800468_1.html)
  - **India-1** (the top segment) accounts for **about two-thirds of discretionary spending**. The affluent segment is "**deepening, not widening**." — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/amp/story/news/indias-main-consuming-class-is-10-of-population-rest-have-no-income-to-spend)
- **UPI scale (NPCI):**
  - **24.51B transactions worth ₹29.82 lakh crore in Aug 2026** (record). July 2026: 23.66B.
  - YoY volume growth slowed to 22% (Aug 2026) from 34% (Aug 2025). Value has plateaued just under ₹30 lakh crore/month since May 2026. — [StartupTalky](https://startuptalky.com/india-upi-transactions-august-2026-record-volume/)
  - May 2026: 23.2B transactions / ₹29.9 lakh crore. — [Open Magazine](https://openthemagazine.com/business/upi-hits-record-232-billion-transactions-in-may-2026-what-the-latest-npci-data-reveals)
- **SaaS/AI from India to global:**
  - Bain: SaaS funding rose ~1.5x and AI-native funding 1.5x in 2025. — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/fintech-saas-drive-indias-vc-investments-to-16-bn-in-2025-log-second-straight-year-of-growth-bain-report/)
  - In Peak XV Surge 12, 13 of 18 startups target global markets. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/peak-xv-goes-bigger-at-seed-with-new-surge-cohort-as-series-a-bar-rises/)
  - Indian B2B SaaS often reports **60–80% of ARR from North America, Europe and ANZ** within ~3 years. — [Deepak Gupta blog](https://guptadeepak.com/the-strategic-blueprint-for-indian-b2b-saas-startups-targeting-us-markets/) *(single blog; low weight)*
- Bain's 2026 outlook focus areas: **AI, quick commerce, clean energy**. Coming-years themes: AI, deeptech, climate, industrial innovation. — [Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/business/indias-vc-landscape-to-shift-to-monetization-profitability-by-2026-articleshow-gqgt3n1); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/fintech-saas-drive-indias-vc-investments-to-16-bn-in-2025-log-second-straight-year-of-growth-bain-report/)

### Inferences
- **Market-sizing guardrail for iPreneur's AI:** it should push bottom-up sizing anchored on a real segment (e.g. "India-1 households ~30M", or "UPI-transacting merchants") and flag top-down "1.4B population" TAMs. A Bharat-tier slider (India-1 / India-2 / India-3, or metro / tier-2 / tier-3) with ARPU per tier fits the Indus Valley framing.
- Default metric set for India decks:
  - Revenue (₹ Cr, FY-labelled)
  - MoM/YoY growth
  - Gross margin
  - **Contribution margin (CM1/CM2/CM3)**
  - CAC
  - **CAC payback (months)**
  - LTV/CAC
  - Monthly burn (₹ lakh/Cr)
  - **Runway (months)**
  - **Months to EBITDA breakeven**
  - Retention/churn
  - For B2B SaaS: ARR in USD, NRR
- UPI's value plateau with rising volume implies falling average ticket size. This matters for fintech ARPU assumptions. Low-ARPU Bharat models need volume and distribution proof.
- Cross-border SaaS decks should report ARR in USD with a geographic revenue split. Domestic consumer decks should report in ₹ crore.

### Gaps
- No 2026 edition of the Indus Valley Report surfaced. Figures above are from the 2025 edition.
- **ONDC** order volumes and India Stack / Account Aggregator metrics for 2026 were not found in this session.
- No authoritative India-specific **ARPU ceilings** by tier, or published contribution-margin benchmarks by sector (quick commerce, D2C, SaaS), were found.
- No source quantified Series A **revenue/ARR thresholds** in India. Peak XV said only that the bar has risen.

## 4. Formatting conventions: crore/lakh vs million/billion, INR vs USD, ₹ notation, FY vs calendar year

### Takeaway
I found no investor-published rule. Indian practitioner guides, data reports and official statistics consistently use **₹ with lakh/crore** (e.g. "₹8.0 Cr", "₹29.82 lakh crore") for domestic figures, often with a USD equivalent alongside. Cross-border and VC-headline reporting uses USD millions/billions. Stating **GST treatment** is an India-specific convention.

### Cited Findings
- A YourStory deck playbook uses rupee figures in crore throughout (e.g. "zero to Rs 50 crore in three years"). — [YourStory, Jan 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/01/pitch-deck-playbook-get-funded)
- FinVal's 2026 India funding report presents medians as **₹ Cr with USD in parentheses** ("₹8.0 Cr (~US$0.8M)"). — [FinVal Research](https://finvalresearch.in/india-startup-funding-report-2026/)
- NPCI/UPI data is reported in **lakh crore** ("₹29.82 lakh crore"). — [StartupTalky](https://startuptalky.com/india-upi-transactions-august-2026-record-volume/)
- Government schemes are denominated in **₹ crore/lakh** (FoF 2.0 "₹10,000 crore"; ELEVATE "up to ₹50 lakh"; SEBI angel fund limits "₹10 lakh to ₹25 crore"). — [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/government-notifies-startup-india-fof-20/print_manually); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/01/karnataka-startup-policy-rs-571-cr-outlay-targets-deeptech-growth-beyond-bengaluru); [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/top-stories/sebi-revises-angel-fund-regulations-accredited-investors-made-mandatory-investment-capped-at-rs-25-crore-1432381)
- Funding-market reports (Bain-IVCA, Tracxn) headline **USD billions**. — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/fintech-saas-drive-indias-vc-investments-to-16-bn-in-2025-log-second-straight-year-of-growth-bain-report/); [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/12/18/dcm91-biz-tech-startups-funding.html)
- State whether figures are **net of GST**. — [Legal Suvidha](https://legalsuvidha.com/blog/crafting-pitch-deck-for-startup)

### Inferences
- The following are general background knowledge, **not sourced in this session**:
  - 1 lakh = 100,000 (₹1,00,000)
  - 1 crore = 10 million (₹1,00,00,000)
  - ₹100 Cr = ₹1 billion
  - Indian digit grouping is 2-2-3 (12,34,56,789)
  - The Indian statutory financial year runs **1 April–31 March**, written as "FY26" = Apr 2025–Mar 2026
- Recommended iPreneur defaults:
  - **(a)** A currency mode toggle: "India investors" (₹ lakh/Cr primary, USD secondary in brackets) vs "Global investors" (USD M primary)
  - **(b)** Always label periods "FY26 (Apr '25–Mar '26)" for Indian decks, and use CY or the US fiscal year for US-facing decks
  - **(c)** A dated FX rate footnote (2026 conversions imply ~₹95/USD per FinVal)
  - **(d)** A "Revenue shown net of GST" footnote
  - **(e)** Use the "₹" glyph or "Rs"/"INR" consistently, with "Cr" as the abbreviation for crore
- A mixed-units deck ("₹8 Cr ARR, $50M TAM") is a common confusion risk. iPreneur's validator could flag mixed units within one slide.

### Gaps
- No primary source (VC blog or investor guideline) states an explicit **preference** between crore and USD millions, or between FY and CY. This is a convention inferred from practice.

## 5. Regulatory and compliance items investors check in India

### Takeaway
Key 2024–2026 changes:
- **Angel tax abolished** (Budget, 23 Jul 2024, all investor classes)
- **SEBI angel fund overhaul** (Sept 2025: accredited investors only, ₹10 lakh–₹25 Cr per startup)
- **DPDP Rules notified** (13 Nov 2025; most obligations from 13 May 2027)
- **RBI payment aggregator directions** (Sept 2025: ₹15 Cr net worth at application, ₹25 Cr by year 3)
- A strong **reverse-flip trend** (Groww, PhonePe, Razorpay, Zepto, Pine Labs, Meesho) as Indian IPOs become the exit route

Baseline diligence items remain DPIIT recognition, entity structure and cap table, FEMA compliance for foreign money, GST, and sector licences.

### Cited Findings
**Angel tax (Section 56(2)(viib))**
- Abolished "for all classes of investors" in the Union Budget 2024–25 (speech 23 Jul 2024). — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/budget-2024-25-fm-nirmala-sitharaman-abolishes-angel-tax); [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/budget/news/budget-2024-abolishes-angel-tax-on-startups-for-all-classes-of-investors-124072300667_1.html); [India TV](https://www.indiatvnews.com/business/budget/budget-2024-centre-abolishes-angel-tax-for-all-classes-of-investors-unlisted-companies-what-it-means-startups-entrepreneurs-updates-2024-07-23-943244)
- Background: inserted 16 Mar 2012. It taxed share premium above fair market value at about 30–30.9% (sources differ). The Finance Act 2023 had extended it to non-resident investors. — [Singhania & Co](https://singhania.in/blog/the-end-of-angel-tax-and-its-implications); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/tax-authorities/1510666/the-end-of-angel-tax-and-its-implications)
- The 2026 founder discourse cites angel-tax abolition as a reason to stay India-domiciled. — [eChai Ventures](https://echai.ventures/startingup/raise-or-not/should-i-raise-from-indian-vcs-or-try-to-get-a-us-or-global-fund-on-my-cap-table)

**DPIIT recognition and tax benefits**
- Eligibility:
  - Turnover ≤ **₹100 Cr** in every FY since incorporation
  - ≤ **10 years** since incorporation
  - Must be a Pvt Ltd, LLP or registered partnership
  - Must not be formed by splitting or reconstructing an existing business — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/faqs-dpiit-recognition-for-start-ups/); [SetIndiaBiz](https://www.setindiabiz.com/blog/dpiit-startup-recognition-eligibility-criteria-guide)
- **Section 80-IAC:** 100% profit deduction for any 3 consecutive years out of the first 10. It needs separate IMB certification. Incorporation cutoff extended to **31 Mar 2030** (Budget 2025), though one older source still says 31 Mar 2025. — [SetIndiaBiz](https://www.setindiabiz.com/blog/section-80-iac-tax-exemption-startups-guide); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/dpiit-startup-recognition-section-80iac-tax-benefits.html); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/dpiit-grants-tax-relief-to-187-startups-under-the-revised-80-iac-provisions)

**FEMA / foreign investment instruments**
- Convertible notes from foreign investors:
  - Startup must be **DPIIT-recognised**
  - Minimum **₹25 lakh per investor per tranche**
  - Must convert or be repaid within **10 years** (extended from the original 5 in the 2017 rules)
  - Sector must be under the 100% FDI automatic route
  - Form CN filed within 30 days — [Squire Patton Boggs (2017 baseline)](https://www.squirepattonboggs.com/~/media/files/insights/publications/2017/02/india-update-issue-of-convertible-notes-by-startup-companies/india-update-issue-of-convertible-notes-by-startup-companies.pdf); [LawSikho](https://lawsikho.com/blog/?p=5851); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=285119) *(secondary; verify against FEMA NDI Rules)*
- RBI notification **FEMA 395(3)/2025-RB (14 Jan 2025)** covers mode of payment and reporting for non-debt instruments. — [TaxTMI](https://www.taxtmi.com/article/detailed?id=16812) *(secondary)*

**Entity structure, flips and reverse flips**
- US institutional investors (YC, most US funds) generally invest only in a **Delaware C-corp**. A flip needs a SEBI-registered Cat I merchant banker valuation of the Indian entity, with transfer at or above FMV. Risks include POEM, transfer pricing and FEMA enforcement. — [eChai Ventures](https://echai.ventures/glossary/startup-flip); [Advisory Monks](https://advisorymonks.pages.dev/insights/flip-structuring-gaar.html); [IncorpX](https://www.incorpx.io/blog/saas-flip-structure-us-india-tax) *(advisory content)*
- Reverse flips completed or underway:
  - **Razorpay** (US parent merged into Indian entity, May 2025) — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/05/razorpay-shifts-domicile-to-india-as-it-preps-for-ipo-reverse-flip)
  - **Zepto** (Singapore→India, NCLT approval) — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/zepto-completes-reverse-flip-from-singapore-to-india-8661011)
  - **Groww** (reported tax cost ₹1,340 Cr) and **PhonePe** (tax liability up to ~$900M) — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/start-up/zeptos-homecoming-gets-nclt-nod-how-indian-start-ups-reverse-flipping-concept-work)
  - **Pine Labs** and **Meesho** — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/shareholders/1667122/homecoming-of-indian-startups-a-deep-dive-into-reverse-ipping)
  - **Conflict:** one source says a 2024 amendment removed the NCLT requirement for inbound cross-border mergers (RBI approval only), but Zepto still went through NCLT. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/start-up/zeptos-homecoming-gets-nclt-nod-how-indian-start-ups-reverse-flipping-concept-work); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/shareholders/1667122/homecoming-of-indian-startups-a-deep-dive-into-reverse-ipping)

**SEBI AIF / Angel Funds (amendment notified ~8–9 Sept 2025)**
- Angel Funds may raise **only from Accredited Investors**.
- The investor minimum ticket was removed.
- Per-startup investment is **₹10 lakh to ₹25 Cr** (previously ₹25 lakh to ₹10 Cr).
- **1-year lock-in**.
- The 25% single-company concentration cap was removed.
- Existing funds had until **8 Sept 2026** to comply. — [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/top-stories/sebi-revises-angel-fund-regulations-accredited-investors-made-mandatory-investment-capped-at-rs-25-crore-1432381); [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/09/12/sebi-revises-angel-fund-regulations-2025-legal-update/); [IndiaCorpLaw](https://indiacorplaw.in/2025/07/15/optimizing-sebis-angel-fund-reforms-accreditation-to-suitability-alignment/)

**DPDP Act 2023 / DPDP Rules 2025**
- Rules notified by MeitY around **13 Nov 2025**, with a staggered rollout:
  - The Data Protection Board is constituted
  - Consent manager obligations apply at **12 months (~13 Nov 2026)**
  - Core data-fiduciary obligations apply at **18 months (~13 May 2027)**: notice, security safeguards, breach reporting, retention, children's data, Significant Data Fiduciary duties, cross-border transfers — [SS Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/meity-notifies-final-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025/); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/breaking-govt-notifies-final-dpdp-rules-sets-staggered-rollout-with-key-obligations-for-data-fiduciaries-84201.htm); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1775140/from-draft-to-reality-key-changes-in-indias-dpdp-rules-2025)
- No startup exemption was found in the coverage. Sources conflict on when Rule 4 (Board functioning) takes effect. — [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_in/insights/cybersecurity/transforming-data-privacy-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025)

**RBI rules for fintech**
- RBI Payment Aggregator Directions (Sept 2025, effective immediately):
  - Non-bank PAs need **₹15 Cr net worth at application** and **₹25 Cr by end of the 3rd FY** after authorisation
  - CCPS count toward net worth; DTAs are excluded
  - Banks do not need separate authorisation
  - Reported deadlines: apply by **31 Dec 2025** or wind down by **28 Feb 2026** — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/rbi-issues-new-master-directions-for-payment-aggregators-rules-take-effect-immediately); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=531658); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/financial-services/1726232/rbi-master-directions-2025-compliance-and-operational-challenges-for-payment-aggregators)

**GST**
- Revenue is reconciled to GST returns, so decks should state whether figures are gross or net of GST. — [Legal Suvidha](https://legalsuvidha.com/blog/crafting-pitch-deck-for-startup)

### Inferences
- A "**Compliance and structure**" slide or appendix in iPreneur Indian decks should show:
  - DPIIT recognition number and date
  - Entity type (Pvt Ltd / LLP)
  - Domicile (India / Delaware / Singapore) and any planned flip or reverse flip
  - Simplified cap table (founders %, ESOP pool %, investors)
  - Instruments used (equity / CCPS / CCD / convertible note / iSAFE)
  - Sector licences (RBI PA/NBFC/AA; SEBI; IRDAI)
  - DPDP readiness (consent flows, data localisation, breach process)
  - GST registration
- For fintech decks, iPreneur should prompt for the licence route (own licence vs partner bank/NBFC) and the PA net-worth runway (₹15 Cr → ₹25 Cr). This directly affects use-of-funds.
- Angel tax no longer needs a mitigation slide. Older templates that mention "Section 56 exemption via DPIIT" are outdated.

### Gaps
- **RBI Digital Lending Directions 2025** (and the default loss guarantee and Account Aggregator regimes): not found in this session.
- Exact effective date and assessment-year mechanics of angel tax removal in the Finance (No. 2) Act 2024: news reports say FY2024-25, but the enacted text was not checked.
- **Primary text** of the FEMA NDI Rules on convertible notes and of the 80-IAC 2030 extension was not verified. Sources are secondary.
- FDI sectoral caps and Press Note 3 (2020) on investment from land-bordering countries, which matters for Chinese-linked investors: not researched in this session.

## 6. Government programs and pitch formats (SISFS, SIDBI FoF, state policies, Shark Tank India)

### Takeaway
Government money reaches startups mostly **indirectly**. FoF 2.0 (₹10,000 Cr, notified 13 Apr 2026) invests through SEBI AIFs. SISFS disburses through incubators, which run a pitch-deck-plus-Q&A process. State programmes give non-dilutive grants, e.g. Karnataka ELEVATE up to ₹50 lakh, ELEVATE NxT up to ₹1 Cr for deep tech, and Kerala KSUM idea grant ₹3 lakh. Shark Tank India popularised a consumer-heavy, "₹X lakh for Y% equity" ask format with on-air haggling. Critics say it anchors founders to low valuations.

### Cited Findings
**Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)**
- Eligibility:
  - DPIIT-recognised
  - Incorporated ≤ **2 years** at application
  - Pvt Ltd, LLP or partnership
  - **≥51% Indian promoter holding**
  - Tech-driven, innovative, scalable idea — [Startupflora](https://startupflora.com/post/startup-india-seed-fund-scheme-explained-who-can-apply); [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/startup-india-seed-fund-scheme)
- Instruments: **grants up to ₹20 lakh** (proof of concept and prototype) and **convertible debentures or debt up to ₹50 lakh** (market entry and scale-up), per third-party guides. Sources conflict on the details. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1040488); [Recur Club](https://www.recurclub.com/blog/obtain-seed-funding-startup-india-scheme)

**Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0**
- Corpus **₹10,000 Cr**. Approved 5 Apr 2025 (Piyush Goyal) with a first ₹2,000 Cr tranche to SIDBI. Formally notified **13 Apr 2026**. — [BioSpectrum](https://biospectrumindia.com/news/16/25941/govt-approves-second-fund-of-funds-worth-rs-10000-cr-to-boost-deep-tech-and-early-stage-innovation.html); [IANS Live](https://ianslive.in/govt-launches-rs-10000-crore-startup-india-fof-20-to-boost-innovation-ecosystem--20260413202652)
- Invests via **AIFs only** into **DPIIT-recognised** startups. Priority areas: **deep tech** (AI, robotics, quantum, biotech, precision manufacturing), early growth-stage startups backed by smaller AIFs, and tech-driven manufacturing. SIDBI is the implementing agency, with an AIF-selection committee (VCIC) and an Empowered Committee. — [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/government-notifies-startup-india-fof-20/print_manually); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-govt-rs-10000-cr-fund-of-funds-2-0-deep-tech/); [KNN India](https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/sectors/others/govt-rolls-out-rs-10000-cr-fof-20-to-support-deep-tech-growth-stage-startups)

**State policies**
- **Karnataka Startup Policy 2025–30:**
  - Outlay ₹518.27 Cr per Outlook Business, or ~₹571 Cr per YourStory/Entrepreneur; reports conflict
  - Target of **25,000 startups by 2030**, with a push beyond Bengaluru
  - **ELEVATE**: one-time grant-in-aid up to **₹50 lakh**, no equity taken
  - **ELEVATE NxT**: ₹150 Cr deep-tech programme, milestone grants up to **₹1 Cr per startup**, open to startups across India
  - ELEVATE 2025 had 146 winners, ₹38.85 Cr committed — [YourStory, Jan 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/01/karnataka-startup-policy-rs-571-cr-outlay-targets-deeptech-growth-beyond-bengaluru); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/karnataka-unveils-518-cr-start-up-policy-to-build-25000-ventures-by-2030-push-beyond-bengaluru); [DQ India](https://www.dqindia.com/esdm/karnataka-unveils-startup-policy-2025-30-charts-roadmap-for-deep-tech-decade-11018513)
- **Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM):**
  - **Idea Grant up to ₹3 lakh**; an older ₹2 lakh figure still circulates
  - Then a **Productisation Grant up to ₹7 lakh** (₹12 lakh for women-led startups)
  - A "seed fund" of ₹15 lakh appears on aggregators as a loan, unverified — [Startupflora (2026)](https://startupflora.com/blog/ksum-idea-grant-2026-amount-eligibility-and-how-to-apply-online); [BusinessWorld](https://businessworld.in/article/ksums-innovation-grants-to-startups-crosses-rs-25-cr-427202); [Deccan Chronicle (older)](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/current-affairs/120518/kerala-sets-norms-for-startup-funding.html)

**Shark Tank India**
- Redseer on Seasons 1–2:
  - **~90% of pitches were consumer-facing**
  - Sharks invested ~**₹106 Cr** in total
  - 27 Season-1 startups later raised external funding, worth ~6x their Shark Tank deal value, at ~2.5x their show valuations (most within 1.5 years)
  - Sharks typically secured more equity than founders pitched — [Redseer](https://redseer.com/articles/shark-tank-india-a-win-win-for-startups-and-investors/); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2023/07/shark-tank-india-startups-funding-investors-pitches-redseer-report)
- Critique: the format pushes founders to pitch lower asks and give up more equity. Finshots claims eventual valuations drop ~60% on average (commentary, contested). Example ask format: Moonshine declined "₹1 crore for 2.5%." — [Finshots](https://finshots.in/archive/why-is-everyone-dunking-on-shark-tank-india/); [Redseer](https://redseer.com/articles/shark-tank-india-a-win-win-for-startups-and-investors/)

### Inferences
- iPreneur could offer **pitch-format presets**:
  - **(a)** VC/angel deck, 10–15 core slides plus appendix
  - **(b)** SISFS/incubator deck that emphasises PoC stage, the DPIIT number, the ≥51% Indian holding, milestone-linked use of grant/debt, and an innovation/tech angle
  - **(c)** State grant application (Karnataka ELEVATE, KSUM): non-dilutive, milestone-based budget
  - **(d)** A Shark Tank-style short pitch: "₹X lakh for Y% equity", implied valuation, revenue to date, margins
- An "**Ask**" slide for Indian audiences should compute and show the **implied valuation** from the ₹ amount and equity %, because Shark Tank has made founders and angels think in that format.
- Deep-tech founders should be told that FoF 2.0 capital flows through AIFs that prefer deep tech and manufacturing, so mentioning AIF-fit is useful.

### Gaps
- Official SISFS guidelines and amounts were not fetched from startupindia.gov.in. Amounts above come from third-party guides.
- SIDBI FoF 1.0 cumulative deployment figures were not found.
- Other states (Tamil Nadu, Telangana T-Hub, Gujarat, Maharashtra, UP) were not covered.
- No quantitative study found on how Shark Tank changed angel-network pitch style beyond the Redseer data.

## 7. Pitching Indian investors vs US/global investors from India (cross-border SaaS)

### Takeaway
US investors and US enterprise buyers generally expect a **Delaware C-corp** and USD reporting. Indian investors increasingly accept, and sometimes prefer, **India domicile**: angel tax is gone, Indian IPO exits are working (~$2B in 2025), and big names have reverse-flipped. A flip is costly to unwind (Groww ₹1,340 Cr, PhonePe ~$900M in tax). For a global-market SaaS deck, the decision on domicile and audience drives currency, metrics framing and the compliance slide.

### Cited Findings
- US institutional investors fund **Delaware C-corps** almost exclusively. YC and most US funds will "only wire money into a Delaware C corp." — [eChai Ventures](https://echai.ventures/startingup/raise-or-not/should-i-raise-from-indian-vcs-or-try-to-get-a-us-or-global-fund-on-my-cap-table); [eChai glossary](https://echai.ventures/glossary/startup-flip)
- A US entity clears **US enterprise procurement** faster than an Indian entity. — [eChai Ventures](https://echai.ventures/startingup/selling-enterprise/do-i-need-a-us-or-delaware-entity-to-sell-to-american-enterprises-or-can-i-close-the-deal-from-my-indian-company)
- If flipping, do it early. After revenue, an angel cap table and intercompany IP exist, it requires merchant-banker valuations and FEMA filings. — [eChai Ventures](https://echai.ventures/glossary/startup-flip); [Advisory Monks](https://advisorymonks.pages.dev/insights/flip-structuring-gaar.html)
- In 2026, many founders are choosing to **stay India-domiciled or reverse flip**, citing angel tax abolition and Indian public-market exits. — [eChai Ventures](https://echai.ventures/startingup/raise-or-not/should-i-raise-from-indian-vcs-or-try-to-get-a-us-or-global-fund-on-my-cap-table)
- Reverse-flip tax costs: Groww ~₹1,340 Cr; PhonePe up to ~$900M; investor tax could reach 33–42% in a Zepto-style transaction. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/start-up/zeptos-homecoming-gets-nclt-nod-how-indian-start-ups-reverse-flipping-concept-work)
- Indian investors back global-market companies: **13 of 18** in Peak XV Surge 12 target global markets. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/peak-xv-goes-bigger-at-seed-with-new-surge-cohort-as-series-a-bar-rises/)
- India VC exits: ~$2B in IPO exits in 2025 from eight listings. — [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/vcs-see-2b-from-ipo-exits-in-2025/story)
- Indian B2B SaaS revenue typically ends up 60–80% from North America, Europe and ANZ within ~3 years. — [Deepak Gupta blog](https://guptadeepak.com/the-strategic-blueprint-for-indian-b2b-saas-startups-targeting-us-markets/) *(low weight)*

### Inferences
- Suggested iPreneur "Audience" switch and its effects:

  | Setting | India investors | US/global investors |
  |---|---|---|
  | Currency | ₹ Cr/lakh primary, USD secondary | USD M primary |
  | Period | FY (Apr–Mar) | CY |
  | Profitability emphasis | High: CM, burn, breakeven | Growth efficiency: ARR growth, NRR, burn multiple |
  | Market sizing | India-1/2/3, Bharat tiers | Global TAM, ICP by geography |
  | Compliance slide | DPIIT, Pvt Ltd, FEMA, DPDP | Delaware C-corp, Indian subsidiary, IP assignment, transfer pricing |
  | Exit slide | Indian IPO / strategic | US M&A / Nasdaq |

- For cross-border SaaS raising from Indian VCs (e.g. Peak XV, Accel, Elevation), a hybrid works: USD ARR and NRR, plus ₹ burn/runway because the cost base is in India. This is effectively "India cost arbitrage" as a capital-efficiency story.

### Gaps
- No source directly compares **Series A ARR thresholds** expected by US vs Indian VCs for India-origin SaaS.
- No primary investor-published guidance (e.g. from Accel India or Elevation) on domicile preference in 2026 was found. Evidence is founder and advisory-firm commentary.
- US-side legal requirements (409A, QSBS eligibility for Delaware flips with Indian founders) were not researched.

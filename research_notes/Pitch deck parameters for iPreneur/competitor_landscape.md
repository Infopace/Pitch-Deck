# Competitive Landscape: AI Pitch Deck Generators and Presentation Tools (as of 27 Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-27. Live pricing pages fetched today: Beautiful.ai (worked) and Slidebean (worked). gamma.app/pricing returned HTTP 403 and pitchbob.io/pricing returned 404, so Gamma and PitchBob prices below come from third-party trackers dated 2026. All prices are USD unless marked otherwise. Many review and comparison sources are written by competing vendors (Presentations.ai, Plus AI, PageOn, Zemith, Prezent, Deckary, Peony, eesel). This is flagged inline where it matters.

## Q1. Who are the main competitors, and what does each one offer and stand for?

### Takeaway
The market splits into five groups:
1. **Horizontal AI deck generators**: Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Presentations.ai, Decktopus, Plus AI, SlidesAI. Gamma leads this group by a wide margin.
2. **Incumbent suites that now include AI**: Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint, Gemini in Google Slides, Canva.
3. **General AI agents that output .pptx**: ChatGPT, Claude, Genspark.
4. **Startup and fundraising specialists**: Slidebean, PitchBob, Upmetrics.
5. **Deck-sharing and investor-relations tools**: DocSend, Visible.vc, Storydoc, Pitch.com.

Tome no longer exists as a deck product. Its presentation app shut down on 30 April 2025, and the team pivoted to Lightfield, an AI CRM.

### Cited Findings

**Horizontal AI deck generators**

  - It raised a $68M Series B at a $2.1B valuation, led by Andreessen Horowitz with Accel and Uncork participating. The round included $20M of employee secondary.
  - The company says it passed $100M ARR, has been profitable for more than two years, and reached that scale on $23M of initial funding with about 50 staff.
  - Sources: [BusinessWire, 10 Nov 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251110805751/en); [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/gamma-68-million-at-2-1-billion-valuation-raised-as-it-surpasses-100-million-in-arr/amp/)

- **Gamma "Agent"** (Gamma 3.0) can research a topic for you. Web research runs only in the Create flow. When the user uploads documents, Gamma treats them as "the source of truth" and cites them in the output "where possible" — [Gamma Help Center](https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/15002203-how-do-i-create-with-agent); [Product Hunt – Gamma 3.0](https://www.producthunt.com/p/gamma-3/gamma-3-0?page=1)
- **Beautiful.ai** sells itself on "Smart Slides" that design themselves.
  - Features: 300+ Smart Slide layouts, unlimited AI generation, AI images, AI writing assistant and translation, PowerPoint import and export, viewer analytics, and team real-time collaboration.
  - The pricing page fetched on 2026-10-27 does not list any pitch-deck-specific templates.
  - Source: [Beautiful.ai pricing (fetched 2026-10-27)](https://www.beautiful.ai/pricing)
- **Presentations.ai** is based in Bengaluru and is the most prominent Indian-origin AI deck generator.
  - It raised a $3M seed round led by Accel in early 2025. Angel investors include Kunal Shah (CRED), Vijay Shekhar Sharma (Paytm), Girish Mathrubootham (Freshworks) and Phanindra Sama (redBus).
  - It claims more than 5M users. Its CEO, Sumanth Raghavendra, says it makes "millions of dollars" in profit; this is not independently verified.
  - It planned to spend the round on a "presentation agent" and enterprise sales.
  - Sources: [TechCrunch, 3 Feb 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/03/accel-backs-indian-ai-startup-building-chatgpt-for-presentations); [Presentations.ai blog](https://www.presentations.ai/blog-cms/presentations-ai-seed-funding-2025)
- **Plus AI** runs inside Google Slides and PowerPoint as an add-in. **SlidesAI** is a Google Slides plugin. **Decktopus** is a standalone platform with analytics and lead capture, which makes it a better fit for sales decks than for everyday slide work — [CostBench Plus AI vs Decktopus](https://costbench.com/compare/decktopus-vs-plus-ai/); [CostBench SlidesAI vs Decktopus](https://costbench.com/compare/decktopus-vs-slidesai/)

**Incumbent suites**

  - It is included in Workspace Business and Enterprise Standard/Plus, Google AI Pro and Ultra, and the AI Expanded Access add-on.
  - Promotional higher limits ran until at least 1 Aug 2026; per-user limits apply after that.
  - Source: [Google Workspace Updates blog](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/06/create-fully-native-and-editable-presentations-with-Gemini-in-Google-Slides.html)
- **Microsoft Copilot** comes bundled with consumer Microsoft 365. In test markets (Australia, New Zealand, Malaysia, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand), Personal and Family plans include 60 AI credits a month, and unused credits do not roll over — [Techloy](https://techloy.com/microsoft-expands-copilot-to-personal-and-family-plans-but-with-a-price-hike)
- Microsoft 365 Premium with Copilot was reported at $19.99/month — [Digit](https://www.digit.in/?p=1167693)
- **Canva** includes the Magic Studio AI toolkit in Pro. Magic Design turns a prompt into a presentation — [Business Today India, Dec 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/canva-review-the-design-platform-that-turned-visual-communication-into-a-utility-507634-2025-12-22)

**General AI agents**
- **Claude and ChatGPT** both output editable .pptx files now. Anthropic documents that Claude creates .pptx, .xlsx, .docx and PDF files.
  - One head-to-head test found Claude won most rounds on content and brand-template fidelity, but "neither produced a slide deck they'd present without cleanup."
  - The source is Plus AI, which sells a competing product.
  - Source: [Plus AI blog](https://plusai.com/blog/chatgpt-vs-claude-for-powerpoint)
- A single-prompt Medium test found ChatGPT's file had plain slides with one bullet each. Claude's file was closer to presentation-ready — [Medium](https://medium.com/no-time/chatgpt-vs-claude-the-powerpoint-test-cd593ac8ecaa)
- **OpenAI acquired NextSlide**, an AI presentation startup that turned prompts, notes, documents or research into editable decks.
  - Founder Ahmed Beshry disclosed the deal on 8 Aug 2026, months after it closed. Terms were not disclosed, and the team now works on ChatGPT.
  - Sources: [Implicator](https://www.implicator.ai/openai-acquires-nextslide-discloses-deal-months-late/); [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/143980-openai-acquires-presentation-startup-nextslide/)
- **Genspark AI Slides** runs on Genspark's "Super Agent." It does the research itself and exports PPTX or PDF.
  - One reviewer says a single deck uses an estimated 250–450 credits, and those credits are shared across all Genspark features.
  - Presentations.ai, a competitor, describes Genspark as a multi-agent workspace, not a dedicated presentation tool.
  - Sources: [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/icymi-genspark-lets-you-create-full-slide-decks-from-a-single-prompt.md); [Presentations.ai review](https://www.presentations.ai/blog/genspark-review)

**Startup and fundraising specialists**
- **Slidebean** pairs an AI deck builder with founder services. Its plans include:
  - 100+ pitch deck templates
  - View tracking
  - "Unlimited AI pitch deck reviews"
  - A cap table tool
  - A financial modeling bootcamp
  - On the $99 Accelerate tier: an Investor Finder and CRM, a financial forecast tool, and monthly human sessions on deck writing and financial modeling

- **Upmetrics** is a business-plan platform. It bundles a pitch deck builder, 400+ sample plans, and 3-year and 7-year financial forecasts, and its Premium plan syncs with QuickBooks Online and Xero — [Startup Savant](https://startupsavant.com/service-reviews/upmetrics)
- **Prezent** (Los Altos, founded 2021 by Rajat Mishra) is an enterprise tool, not a founder tool.
  - Its assistant, "Astrid," uses models trained on about 2M slide decks.
  - It serves about 150 Fortune 2000 companies, mainly in biopharma and tech, and had more than $10M ARR.
  - It raised $20M in March 2025 at a valuation above $100M, led by Greycroft.
  - Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2982365); [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/prezent-raises-20m-for-ai-slides)
  - A later $30M raise is earmarked for acquiring AI services firms, starting with Mishra's Prezentium. I saw only the headline and snippet — [Yahoo Finance/TechCrunch](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/prezent-raises-30-million-acquire-171129596.html)

**Deck-sharing and investor-relations tools**

- **Visible.vc** is an investor-update and fundraising CRM.
  - Its free tier covers updates to 100 investors, 2 pitch decks, 2 pipelines, and access to "Visible Connect."

- **Pitch.com** has moved from general presentations toward sales "pitch rooms." It launched a HubSpot app in February 2025.
  - In January 2024 it returned most of its VC money and cut headcount about 78%, from 180 to 40.
  - Sacra estimates it reached about $10M ARR in February 2025.
  - Sources: [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/pitch); [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/pitch-lays-off-two-third-of-staff)

**India-specific players**

- **PitchDeck (pitchdeck.io)** is listed as a resource partner on the Startup India portal. It offers an AI deck builder, a content chatbot, sharing and tracking, and one free deck for DPIIT-recognized startups — [Startup India portal](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/reources/resource-partners/business-services/PitchDeck.html)

### Inferences
- iPreneur's real competition comes from three directions:
  1. Gamma as the default "good enough" generator.
  2. Bundled AI (Gemini in Slides, Copilot, Canva) that many Indian founders already pay for.
  3. General agents (ChatGPT, Claude, Genspark) that now research, write and export .pptx in a single prompt.
- OpenAI's purchase of NextSlide suggests ChatGPT will get stronger native deck generation, which would raise the baseline every pitch-specific tool has to beat.
- Specialist founder tools (Slidebean, PitchBob, Upmetrics) compete on fundraising workflow, not design. iPreneur's research agents and claims auditor put it in this group. Its closest analogues are Slidebean (deck plus services plus investor CRM) and PitchBob (deck plus scoring).

### Gaps
- I could not open Gamma's live pricing page (HTTP 403) or PitchBob's (404).
- I found no first-party description of Copilot in PowerPoint's generation or grounding features as of late 2026. Only consumer bundling and pricing came up.
- I found no primary 2026 news on Pitch.com's status, such as an acquisition or further pivot. The latest data is Sacra's February 2025 estimate.
- I found no Inc42 or YourStory coverage of Indian AI pitch-deck startups beyond Presentations.ai and the pitchdeck.io portal listing. Other India players may exist that this search missed.

## Q2. What does each competitor charge, and which pitch-deck features does it have?

### Takeaway
- **Pricing tiers:**
  - Mainstream AI deck tools cluster at $8–$25 per user per month.
  - Team plans run $40–$50 per user per month.
  - Specialist tools with services charge about $99/month (Slidebean Accelerate, PitchBob white-label).
  - Deck-sharing and data-room tools charge $10–$300/month (DocSend).
- **INR pricing** turned up only for Canva and Microsoft. No AI pitch-deck specialist I found publishes INR pricing or bills through Indian rails.
- **Feature coverage** is uneven:
  - Analytics or view-tracking and PPTX export are common.
  - Investor CRM and financial forecasting appear only in Slidebean, Upmetrics and Visible.
  - Market-sizing research with verifiable citations is weak or unproven everywhere.

## Q3. Which tools offer deck scoring or AI investor feedback, how credible is it, and which ground content in real research with citations?

### Takeaway
- **Scoring:** Slidebean (unlimited AI reviews), PitchBob (11-criteria VC score) and smaller tools (Pitchleague.ai, PitchGrade) all offer it. None has published validation against real investor outcomes, so the scores are heuristic marketing features, not predictors.
- **Research grounding:** Gamma Agent, Genspark, Gemini in Slides (using the user's Drive files) and general agents all ground content in research. Gamma's web citations have been reported, by a competitor, as fabricated. None of these tools offers a claim-by-claim audit trail.

### Cited Findings
- **PitchBob** scores decks on 11 VC-grade criteria. This is the vendor's own claim — [PitchBob](https://pitchbob.io/products/vc)
  - Product Hunt rating: 2.6 from 7 reviews. Complaints cite poor structure, spelling errors and irrelevant content — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/pitchbob-io/reviews?page=2)
  - A third-party listing reports Trustpilot complaints about spelling errors, misaligned slides and generic AI output — [zPlatform](https://zplatform.ai/lifetime-deals/pitchbob-ai-pitch-deck-generator/)
  - A third-party listing also carries an unverified allegation that PitchBob shared user decks with its investor network after users declined. I could not confirm this on Trustpilot itself.
- **Slidebean** includes "Unlimited AI pitch deck reviews" from the $7 Starter tier and above, plus human deck-writing review on the $99 tier — [Slidebean pricing](https://slidebean.com/pricing)
- **Pitchleague.ai** scores PDF decks slide by slide, ranks them publicly and benchmarks iterations. **PitchGrade** scores decks on six dimensions. Neither has independent accuracy data, and the listing sites are low quality (one describes "on-chain tokenized scoring") — [TopAI.tools – Pitch Deck Scoring](https://topai.tools/s/pitch-deck-scoring)
- **No validated scoring:** I found no source that compares any tool's deck scores against actual investor decisions or funding outcomes (search across PitchBob, Slidebean and scoring directories) — [TopAI.tools](https://topai.tools/s/pitch-deck-scoring); [ToolsForHumans – Slidebean](https://toolsforhumans.ai/ai-tools/slidebean)
- **Gamma Agent** can run web research in the Create flow. It cites uploaded documents "where possible" — [Gamma Help Center](https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/15002203-how-do-i-create-with-agent)
  - A competitor (PageOn.ai) reports that the web sources Gamma cited "looked plausible but weren't traceable to real articles." That is a single interested source — [PageOn](https://www.pageon.ai/blog/gamma-vs-pageon)
- **Genspark** marketing-style reviews claim multi-AI cross-checking and a "90% hallucination reduction." The figure comes from one unofficial review site and is not confirmed by Genspark — [Gendeal review](https://gendeal-cf6.pages.dev/genspark-ai-slides-review); [Lindy](https://www.lindy.ai/blog/genspark-ai-features)
- **Gemini in Google Slides** grounds generation in the user's own Drive files, not in external market data — [Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/06/create-fully-native-and-editable-presentations-with-Gemini-in-Google-Slides.html)
- **Prezent's Astrid** suggests content from a model trained on about 2M slide decks. That is pattern-matching to past decks, not market-data sourcing — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2982365)
- **What investors penalize:** practitioner sources say it is generic, ungrounded content, not AI use itself.
  - AI defaults to top-down TAM ("$400B market, we only need 1%"), which they call the most ridiculed line in venture.
  - AI projections often "don't connect to pricing, funnel, or burn."
  - Sources (vendor and practitioner blogs): [Peony](https://www.peony.ink/blog/ai-pitch-deck-guide); [Avvale](https://avvale.co.uk/pages/why-investors-reject-ai-pitch-decks); [SearchFunder (investor post)](https://searchfunder.com/post/investor-perspective-ai-generated-pitch-decks-really-hurt-your-credibility)

### Inferences
- A **claims auditor** that tags each numeric claim as sourced, user-provided or unverified, links to the source, and blocks or flags uncited TAM, growth or competitor numbers addresses the most-cited investor complaint directly. No competitor documents an equivalent feature.
- **Scoring credibility** is a weak spot across the market. iPreneur could stand out by making scoring transparent: show the rubric and tie each deduction to a specific slide and claim. It should avoid presenting a single "investability score" as predictive.
- **Bottom-up market sizing** that shows its inputs (customer count × price × reachable share, each sourced) answers the "$400B / 1%" criticism. No competitor advertises it.

### Gaps
- I found no independent benchmark of citation accuracy for Gamma, Genspark, ChatGPT or Claude deck generation.
- I could not find the PitchBob or Slidebean scoring rubrics or any validation studies.
- Whether Copilot in PowerPoint cites web sources in generated decks remains unknown.

## Q4. What do users complain about in reviews?

### Takeaway
The most consistent complaints, in rough order:
1. PowerPoint export breaks layouts (especially Gamma).
2. Generic, look-alike structure and design.
3. Credit limits and costs on paid plans.
4. Invented or unsourced numbers and citations.
5. Spelling and quality problems in niche pitch tools (PitchBob).

General agents (ChatGPT, Claude) still need cleanup before a deck is presentable.

### Cited Findings
- **Gamma PPTX export** is its most common complaint: layout shifts, font substitution and moving text boxes. Its card-based layouts do not convert cleanly to fixed 16:9 slides — [eesel.ai Gamma reviews](https://www.eesel.ai/en/blog/gamma-reviews); [Zemith Gamma review](https://www.zemith.com/en/learn/gamma-review)
  - A consultant-focused review says one 15-slide Gamma deck took over an hour to fix before it was client-ready. Deckary sells a competing product — [Deckary](https://deckary.com/blog/gamma-review)
- **Gamma ratings diverge sharply** by platform. Zemith, a competitor, reports G2 at about 4.5/5 from 150+ reviews and Trustpilot at about 1.5/5 from 410+ reviews. Neither figure was confirmed on G2 or Trustpilot directly — [Zemith](https://www.zemith.com/en/learn/gamma-review)
- **Product Hunt complaints about Gamma**: limited customization, uneven AI accuracy, export and formatting issues, and limited AI credits on paid plans — [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/en/blog/gamma-reviews); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/gamma-3/shoutouts)
- **Gamma's own feedback boards** carry user posts titled "Strong Start, but Limited Long-Term Value for Paying Users" and "I'm not satisfied" — [Gamma Canny](https://meetgamma.canny.io/ideas/p/gamma-review-strong-start-but-limited-long-term-value-for-paying-users); [Gamma ideas board](https://ideas.gamma.app/ideas/p/nao-estou-satisfeito)

Investors reportedly spot template output from identical structures and stock imagery — [Pi blog](https://blog.pi.inc/blog/can-ai-actually-build-a-pitch-deck-that-gets-you-a-meeting); [Peony](https://www.peony.ink/blog/ai-pitch-deck-guide)

  - Tool-use-limit errors forced re-prompting.
  - A small font change used about 18% of session usage.

### Inferences
- **High-fidelity, native PPTX and Google Slides export** is a real differentiator, because the market leader's export is its most criticized feature. Indian investors and accelerators often ask for PPTX or PDF attachments.
- **Credit anxiety** is a recurring complaint. Clear per-deck pricing in INR, with regeneration and audit included, could become a positioning point.
- **Generic sameness** comes from horizontal tools that do not know the stage or sector. iPreneur's sector-aware templates and research agents target this directly.

### Gaps
- No primary Reddit threads (r/startups, r/Entrepreneur) appeared in search. All complaint evidence is from review aggregators, vendor blogs, Product Hunt and Gamma's own feedback boards.
- G2 and Capterra scores were not verified first-hand. Many of the critical write-ups come from competitors.

## Q5. What is the market size, what have competitors raised, and has the market consolidated?

- **Market size:** the "AI presentation generation" segment is estimated at about $1.5–$1.9B (2024–25), growing about 25% a year to about $4.8B by 2029. Estimates vary widely by definition.

## Q6. Where is the white space for iPreneur?

### Takeaway
No competitor found combines all of the following:
- Research grounded in verifiable, cited sources
- A claim-level fact-check audit
- Stage-aware and sector-aware investor structure
- A linked bottom-up financial and market-sizing model
- India-native formats and billing: INR, crore/lakh notation, Indian investor and accelerator norms, Razorpay

The largest gaps are verifiable data, credible and transparent scoring, and India localization. Investor CRM and data-room features already exist in DocSend and Visible, so integrating with them may beat rebuilding them.

### Cited Findings
- **Verified data gap:** investors penalize unsupported top-down TAM figures and projections disconnected from pricing, funnel and burn — [Peony](https://www.peony.ink/blog/ai-pitch-deck-guide); [Avvale](https://avvale.co.uk/pages/why-investors-reject-ai-pitch-decks)
  - Leading generators' citations are either limited to uploaded documents (Gamma "where possible") or disputed as fabricated (Gamma web research, per PageOn) — [Gamma Help](https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/15002203-how-do-i-create-with-agent); [PageOn](https://www.pageon.ai/blog/gamma-vs-pageon)
- **Scoring credibility gap:** scoring tools exist (PitchBob, Slidebean, Pitchleague.ai, PitchGrade), but none publishes validation, and PitchBob's community reviews are poor (2.6/5 on Product Hunt) — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/pitchbob-io/reviews?page=2); [TopAI.tools](https://topai.tools/s/pitch-deck-scoring)
- **Investor workflow is fragmented across tools:**
  - DocSend: data rooms, NDA gating and analytics, $10–$300/month — [DocSend](https://www.docsend.com/pricing/)
  - Visible: investor database, pipelines and updates — [StartupOwl](https://startupowl.com/reviews/visible-vc)
  - Slidebean: Investor Finder and CRM without contacts, at $99/month — [Slidebean](https://slidebean.com/pricing)
  - Pitch.com's HubSpot "pitch room" integration shows the CRM-attached deck pattern on the sales side — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/pitch)
- **India gap:**
  - The only Indian-origin generator with notable funding (Presentations.ai) is horizontal and not pitch-specific — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/03/accel-backs-indian-ai-startup-building-chatgpt-for-presentations)
  - The Startup India portal lists one pitch-deck vendor (pitchdeck.io) offering a free deck to recognized startups — [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/reources/resource-partners/business-services/PitchDeck.html)
  - Canva's 2026 India price change (Pro now one user at about ₹4,000/year, Teams per seat) shows Indian users are price-sensitive to per-seat changes — [Dhanam Online](https://english.dhanamonline.com/news/canva-hikes-global-subscription-prices-by-300-what-does-It-mean-for-indian-users-6949199)
- **Language gap:** Gemini in Slides launched English-only — [Google Workspace blog](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/06/create-fully-native-and-editable-presentations-with-Gemini-in-Google-Slides.html)
- **Export fidelity gap:** the leader's PPTX export is widely criticized — [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/en/blog/gamma-reviews)

### Inferences
These are positioning hypotheses for iPreneur, not sourced facts:
1. **"Every number has a source."** Use the claims auditor as the headline feature. Show a per-slide sources panel and confidence badges, and export an appendix of citations so an investor can check. No competitor documents this end to end.
2. **Stage-aware and sector-aware generation.** Pre-seed, seed and Series A decks differ in what investors expect (traction depth, unit economics, use of funds), as do sectors (SaaS, D2C, fintech, deeptech). Horizontal tools generate one generic sequence, which is the sameness complaint in Q4.
3. **Bottom-up TAM/SAM/SOM builder** with sourced inputs, tied to a simple financial model, so projections connect to pricing and funnel. This answers the most-cited investor criticism.
4. **India-native fundraising:**
   - INR and crore/lakh notation alongside USD
   - Indian market data sources (for example government and RBI statistics, industry bodies)
   - Formats suited to Indian angel networks, accelerators and Startup India / DPIIT applications
   - Razorpay INR billing with per-deck or low monthly pricing set against Canva's roughly ₹333/month effective price
5. **Transparent scoring:** a rubric-based review that cites the slide and claim behind each deduction, explicitly not a "probability of funding."
6. **Integrate rather than rebuild** for distribution: export to PPTX and Google Slides with high fidelity, and share through DocSend or Visible links, or add lightweight view analytics. A full data room is already well served by DocSend and Visible.
7. **Defensive note:** generic generation is being commoditized by Google (June 2026), Microsoft, OpenAI (NextSlide) and Claude. iPreneur's moat has to be verification depth and fundraising workflow, not slide design.

### Gaps
- I found no source documenting whether any competitor supports crore/lakh number formatting or Indian investor-specific templates. The white-space claim rests on absence in search results, not confirmed absence.
- I found no survey of Indian investors' deck-format preferences, such as whether Indian VCs and angels prefer PDF, PPTX or DocSend links.
- I found no data on how many Indian founders use Gamma, Canva or Copilot to make pitch decks.
